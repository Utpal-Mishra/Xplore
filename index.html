Pilot Boundary

XPLORE v0.4.0 adds sampled Route Segment Intelligence across Ireland. Selected routes are matched against nearby OpenStreetMap road, cycling, green-space, foot/sidewalk, lighting and accessibility tags. These measurements are explicitly labelled by data confidence and are not yet included in the XPLORE Score while the matching approach is being validated. Address selection remains confirmation-first and public geocoder/context services remain low-volume development adapters. Live location is processed in the browser and precise coordinates are sent to route/context providers only when a journey or reroute is requested. Journey guidance remains an experimental browser pilot and must not be treated as safety-critical navigation.

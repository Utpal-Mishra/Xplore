## Risk & Assumption Log

**RISK-20261119-V08-01 — Sparse map tags interpreted as absence**  
Impact: High for accessibility/lighting.  
Mitigation: unknown remains unknown; accessibility not scored; lighting requires >=50% known-tag coverage.  
Status: MITIGATED.

**RISK-20261119-V08-02 — Sampled context overstates full-route conditions**  
Impact: Medium.  
Mitigation: evidence gate, route-length thresholds and long-route attenuation.  
Status: MITIGATED.

**RISK-20261119-V08-03 — Context component overwhelms base decision model**  
Impact: Medium.  
Mitigation: maximum preference-specific weights from 8% to 20%; medium evidence attenuated further.  
Status: MITIGATED.

**RISK-20261119-V08-04 — Route Intelligence service outage breaks routing**  
Impact: High if coupled.  
Mitigation: candidate retains base score and route planning continues.  
Status: MITIGATED.

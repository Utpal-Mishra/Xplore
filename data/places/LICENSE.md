# Cork OpenStreetMap place extract

`cork-osm.json` contains data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:

https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The source, query, bounding box, OSM base timestamp and extraction timestamp are included in the dataset. Coordinates for ways/relations are geometric centres; they are not verified entrances. Data completeness, business operation and opening hours are not asserted.

The Desi Bites address correction is kept separately in the search module. Its business address source is https://desibites.ie/contact and the approximate estate road location is https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/75862158 . No Google Maps business database has been copied into this extract.
